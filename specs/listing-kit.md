## Full description (Google Business Profile allows 750 characters)

MCI Eyelashes is a direct eyelash factory in Purbalingga, Central Java, Indonesia. We are a manufacturer, not a trading company, and we sell wholesale to salons, distributors and beauty brands worldwide.

Our catalogue has 236 strip lash styles in four collections: Soft Touch (Korean synthetic fiber), 3D Luxe (multi-layer volume), Faux Mink (protein silk, cruelty free) and Human Hair Classic (100% sterilized human hair).

The minimum order is 100 pairs per style, and you can mix styles to reach it. Samples of catalogue styles are free; you pay shipping. A 100-pair order is produced in 5 working days. We also make OEM and private label lashes with your own packaging, from the same 100-pair minimum.

You can ask for a live video call to see the production floor before you order.

## Standard answers (for the Q&A or "terms" fields)

- **Minimum order:** 100 pairs per style. Styles can be mixed in one order.
- **Samples:** free on existing catalogue styles. The buyer pays shipping.
- **Production time:** 5 working days for a 100-pair order. Larger orders: ask for a timeline.
- **Shipping:** worldwide, 7 to 14 business days.
- **Prices:** choose the styles you want from the catalogue first, then send the style codes. We send the price for those styles.
- **Private label and packaging:** same 100-pair minimum.
- **Factory check:** live video call to the production floor on request.

## Photos to upload

Use these files from the site, in this order: the logo (`public/logo-mci-192.webp`),
the lash photos in `public/products/`, and the packaging picture
(`public/product-packaging-square.webp`). Google Business Profile and Alibaba both
rank listings with real factory photos higher, so add real floor and team photos
as soon as there are any. Do not upload the robot campaign pictures as "factory
photos": directories reject AI images in that slot.

## Do not put in any listing

These were removed from the website because they are not confirmed. A listing that
says them will contradict the site.

- Any certificate (ISO, CE, FDA, PETA and similar)
- "50 years", a founding year, or a staff count that the owner has not confirmed
- Real mink, coloured lashes, 5D fans, or Under Lashes
- Customer counts, country counts, testimonials

## After a listing is live

Send the listing's address so it can be added to the website's company data
(the `sameAs` list in `index.html`). That link in both directions is what tells
search engines the listing and the website are the same company.
