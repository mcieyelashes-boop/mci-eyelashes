# MCI Eyelashes: listing kit

Ready-to-paste text for trade directories and LinkedIn. Every line here matches the
website and was confirmed by the owner. Use exactly the same name, phone and website
on every site: search engines and AI models match listings by those, and small
differences split the credit.

## What MCI is (and what it must not be called)

MCI Eyelashes is a one-person eyelash supplier in Purbalingga, established in 2019.
The lashes are made by partner workshops in Purbalingga that MCI manages. MCI does
not own a factory or an office.

So in every listing:

- Choose business type **Trading Company**, **Supplier** or **Exporter**, never
  **Manufacturer** or **Factory**. Alibaba and similar sites check this against
  business documents, and a wrong type gets a listing rejected or removed.
- Never write "factory direct", "our factory", "own production floor", "in-house"
  or "not a trading company".

## Where to list, with no office

- **Google Business Profile and Bing Places: skip them.** Both require a real place
  where customers can visit, or a business that travels to customers. An online
  export business with no premises does not qualify. A virtual office or a
  made-up address breaks their rules and gets the profile suspended.
- **LinkedIn company page:** no address needed. Do this first; it is free and fast.
- **Indotrading, Europages, TradeIndia-style export directories:** most accept a
  city without a street address.
- **Alibaba:** needs Indonesian business registration (an NIB). If MCI has one, the
  address on the NIB is the address to use. If not, leave Alibaba for later.
- **Instagram and WhatsApp Business:** already in use. Make sure the profile text
  below matches.

## Full description

MCI Eyelashes is an eyelash supplier in Purbalingga, Central Java, Indonesia, established in 2019. Our lashes are made by partner workshops in Purbalingga that we manage, and we sell wholesale to salons, distributors and beauty brands worldwide. You deal with one contact from first sample to shipment.

Our catalogue has 234 strip lash styles in four collections: Soft Touch (Korean synthetic fiber), 3D Luxe (multi-layer volume), Faux Mink (protein silk, cruelty free) and Human Hair Classic (100% sterilized human hair).

The minimum order is 100 pairs per style, and you can mix styles to reach it. Samples of catalogue styles are free; you pay shipping. A 100-pair order is produced in 5 working days. We also supply OEM and private label lashes with your own packaging, from the same 100-pair minimum.

You can ask for a live video call from the partner workshop floor before you order.

## Do not put in any listing

- "Factory", "factory direct", "manufacturer", "own floor", "in-house", "not a trading company"
- Any certificate (ISO, CE, FDA, PETA and similar)
- "50 years", "bestseller", "most popular", "world-class", staff counts
- Real mink, coloured lashes, 5D fans, or Under Lashes
- Customer counts, country counts, testimonials

## After a listing is live

Send the listing's address so it can be added to the website's company data
(the `sameAs` list in `index.html`). That link in both directions is what tells
search engines the listing and the website are the same business.
