---

## Why These Are Different From The Old Sequences

The previous cold-email-sequences.md was written for total strangers.  
Your 90 leads are people who already know MCI — so that tone was wrong.

These sequences treat them as warm contacts: no pitch in email 1,  
no feature dumps, no ISO/CE/FDA stacking, no dollar figures in body copy.

---

## Deliverability Rules Applied (Fix For 38% Spam Rate)

Before sending anything:

1. **Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC** at mxtoolbox.com for mci-eyelashes.com
2. **Use a subdomain**: send from `outreach.mci-eyelashes.com` not main domain
3. **Plain text only** — no HTML, no logos, no tracking pixels in cold sends
4. **Max 50 emails/day** — do not blast all 90 at once
5. **Warm up the subdomain** — start at 10/day, add 5/day each week
6. **Best send day**: Thursday, 9–11am recipient local time
7. **Verify list** with NeverBounce or ZeroBounce before sending — remove bounces

Subject line rules applied:
- 2–4 words, lowercase, no punctuation gimmicks
- No numbers, no currency, no percentages in subject
- "closing the loop" is dead — never use it
